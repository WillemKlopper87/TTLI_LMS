### 1. Architectural Impacts of Your Answers

#### A. Multi-Tenant White-Labeling (`company1.executivetrainingportal.co.za`)
To support custom subdomains and uniquely catered content for big enterprise clients, the system must be built as a **Multi-Tenant SaaS**.
*   **Tenant Resolution:** When a user visits `company1.executivetrainingportal.co.za`, a reverse proxy (e.g., AWS ALB, Nginx, or Traefik) reads the URL, identifies the "Tenant ID", and passes it to the backend.
*   **Customization Engine:** The backend uses the Tenant ID to load specific configurations: custom color palettes, logos, specific course catalogs, and custom registration fields.
*   **SSO Integration:** Enterprise tenants will need SAML/OIDC SSO configured per tenant so their employees can log in using their own corporate credentials (e.g., Microsoft Entra ID).

#### B. South African Data Residency & AI Providers
You require data to reside in South Africa and want to use top-tier AI (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot/Azure OpenAI).
*   **The Residency Challenge:** Not all AI providers have data centers in South Africa. Sending personal data to US/EU servers might violate strict corporate compliance or POPIA interpretations regarding cross-border data flows.
*   **The Solution (Anonymization Pipeline):** Since you specified AI analysis must be *anonymized*, we will build a **PII-Redaction Gateway**. Before any survey, assessment, or feedback text is sent to an LLM, a local middleware service strips names, company names, emails, and specific identifiers, replacing them with synthetic tokens (e.g., `[USER_1]`, `[COMPANY_A]`).
*   **Provider Recommendation:** **Microsoft Azure OpenAI** is currently the strongest choice because Azure has a physical data center region in **South Africa North (Johannesburg)**. This allows you to use enterprise-grade AI while keeping data strictly within SA borders.

#### C. Video Protection & DRM
Standard video hosting is not enough to prevent scraping. You need **Hollywood-grade DRM (Digital Rights Management)**.
*   **Technology:** We must use **Widevine** (for Android/Chrome) and **FairPlay** (for iOS/Safari).
*   **Dynamic Watermarking:** The video player will overlay the logged-in user’s email address and IP address semi-transparently across the video. If someone screen-records it, the leak is traced back to them.
*   **Provider:** Standard S3 buckets cannot do DRM natively. We will use a specialized video pipeline like **AWS Elemental MediaPackage + CloudFront DRM**, or a SaaS like **Mux** or **JWPlayer** configured for DRM and geo-blocking.

#### D. Financial Compliance & Auditing
Since the company is fully VAT registered and requires strict auditing:
*   **Immutable Ledger:** The database must use an append-only ledger approach for financial transactions. Invoices cannot be "deleted", only "voided" via credit notes.
*   **Sequential Numbering:** Invoice numbers must be strictly sequential with no gaps, as required by SARS.
*   **Tax Engine:** The checkout must calculate SA VAT (15%) for local buyers, and handle zero-rated VAT for international exports (USD), requiring the buyer to declare their location/status.

### 5. Specific Workflow: The Anonymized AI Insight Engine

Since AI must be anonymized, here is the exact workflow we will build for **Gate 8**:

1.  **Data Ingestion:** A user submits a leadership assessment or survey containing text: *"I feel my manager, John, doesn't listen to my ideas about the Q3 budget."*
2.  **Local Processing (SA Servers):** Before the text leaves your secure environment, a local NLP script scans it.
3.  **PII Redaction:**
    *   "John" -> `[PERSON_1]`
    *   "Q3 budget" -> `[PROJECT_A]`
    *   User ID is detached from the text payload.
4.  **AI Prompting:** The system sends the sanitized text to Azure OpenAI: *"Analyze the sentiment and themes of this employee feedback: 'I feel my manager, [PERSON_1], doesn't listen to my ideas about [PROJECT_A].'"*
5.  **AI Response:** AI returns: *"Theme: Management Communication. Sentiment: Frustrated. Recommendation: Leadership active-listening training."*
6.  **Recombination:** The system stores this insight in the analytics database, linked to the *aggregate* cohort, not the individual user.
7.  **Dashboard View:** The executive sees the trend, but the individual's identity remains mathematically protected.
