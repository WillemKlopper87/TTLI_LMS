Built on nearly two decades of collaborating with leaders across
industries, Lead with Intent enables organisations with practical
leadership principles that drive engagement and performance.
A Proven 8-Phase Journey:
PHASE 1: DISCOVERY - Gain clear insight through a 360°
leadership assessment, capturing anonymous peer feedback
against the Lead with Intent framework.
PHASE 2: UNDERSTAND - One-on-one discussions unpack
individual results, current realities, and key leadership
challenges. 
PHASES 3-6: DEVELOP - Four focused workshops embed the
core leadership principles and practices that drive engagement
and performance.
PHASE 7: PROGRESS MEASUREMENT - A second 360°
assessment tracks growth and highlights areas of improvement.
PHASE 8: GROWTH PATH - Final one-on-one sessions
consolidate insights, reinforcing progress and future focus.
OUTCOME - Leaders who are more intentional, more aligned,
and better equipped to build engaged, high-performing teams.
